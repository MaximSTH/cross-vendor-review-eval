## The report (verbatim)

**Verification headline first:** the LOCKED efficacy constants are **correct**. `boundary_constants.R` calls `gsDesign(k=4, test.type=2, alpha=0.025, sfu=sfLDOF, timing=c(.25,.5,.75,1))`, which is the right invocation for a two-sided symmetric 0.05 FWER sfLDOF design (gsDesign's `alpha` is per-side); the `.R.out` bounds 4.3326 / 2.9631 / 2.3590 / 2.0141 match the published Lan-DeMets OBF-spending values for K=4 equal spacing, the independent Python recursion reproduces them to <1e-3 with cumulative one-sided α = 0.024987, and the nominal p's in §3.3 are consistent. The efficacy lock survives adversarial checking. Almost nothing built **on top of** those constants does, as follows.

### Numbered findings

**1. BLOCKING — The sequential machinery degenerates at the plausible event rates; the information basis is internally contradictory.**
§3.1 correctly states that information for a McNemar endpoint accrues with the **discordant-pair count**, but §3.2 then schedules looks at case counts n ≈ 11/22/33/44 with I_max implicitly = 44 cases, and the Z-boundaries are Brownian-scale constants. Direct answer to the posed question: **yes, McNemar-based sequential monitoring degenerates when both arms are near zero.** The test statistic lives entirely on discordant pairs; at pilot magnitudes the A2-vs-B discordant count in the pilot was **0 out of 2** defective cases (pos2 both arms mechanical-catch, pos5 both no-catch — concordant both times). Concretely, under the null the exact attainable p with m discordant pairs is 2·2⁻ᵐ at a perfect split, so: the look-1 boundary (nominal p = 1.47×10⁻⁵) is unreachable with fewer than **18** discordant pairs all in one direction — impossible at n=11 under any plausible rates; even the final boundary (p = 0.044) requires **m ≥ 6 with a 6–0 split**. If both arms sit near zero (the futility scenario the design itself calls most likely), m at n_max=44 may be single-digit, the asymptotic Z is meaningless, and "information fraction t" never approaches 1 on the scale the statistic actually lives on. **Standard remedy (pre-register it):** (i) define I_max in **discordant pairs** (m_max), not cases — n_max=44 becomes an administrative accrual cap; (ii) monitor the **exact conditional binomial** (sign test): given m discordant pairs, b ~ Bin(m, ½) under H0 — a group-sequential exact binomial design, implementing the sfLDOF spending via exact tail probabilities ≤ the nominal spend at each look (conservative, standard for discrete outcomes); (iii) schedule looks at t = m/m_max, which Lan-DeMets accommodates by construction; (iv) handle under-running (n=44 exhausted with m < m_max) by the standard Lan-DeMets over/under-running rule — spend all remaining α at the final analysis at the realized t. The locked Z-constants remain usable as the spending targets; what must change is the statistic compared against them and the definition of t.

**2. BLOCKING — The "powered for a >20 pp delta" claim (D-054b basis, §3.2, report §8) is not a paired power computation and is materially wrong.**
It derives from per-arm Wilson half-widths (±15 pp), which is an estimation statement about a one-sample rate, not the power of the paired test. Actual McNemar power at n=44 for a 20 pp paired delta (e.g., 0.15 vs 0.35, independence-model discordance ≈ 0.395, expected drift ≈ 0.20·√(44/0.395) ≈ 2.11 vs a final bound of 2.01) is roughly **50–55%**, lower still with exact-test discreteness. The design has ~90% power only around a **~30 pp** delta with a near-zero same-vendor arm (0.05 vs 0.35 → drift ≈ 3.29) — i.e., roughly the diff-anchoring magnitude the pilot suggests, so the design is defensible, but the pre-registration cannot ship asserting 20 pp power. This also forces the futility calibration (below): calibrating futility at (20 pp, 80–90%) would be incoherent, because β-spending drawn against an alternative the design cannot detect produces futility bounds that recommend stopping on data fully consistent with a true 20 pp effect. Fix: replace the power claim with an honest paired-exact power table under a pre-registered nuisance (discordance) model, and state 20 pp effects as detectable only descriptively.

**3. SHOULD-FIX — The committed gsDesign futility computation is the wrong design; the "reconciliation" is that the R script does not compute β-spending at all.**
`boundary_constants.R` line 23 uses `test.type = 6`, which in gsDesign is a lower bound spent **under H0** (astar-spending), not β-spending under the alternative — that is why its futility bounds (1.8741 / 1.5746 / 1.6424 / 2.0141, non-monotone, absurdly high at look 1) bear no resemblance to the Python β-spending set (−0.94 / 0.45 / 1.22 / 1.79). The ruled concept (D-054e, "β-spending futility, non-binding") corresponds to **`test.type = 4`** (non-binding futility, β-spending), under which the efficacy bounds stay exactly the locked values. Additionally, the printed "max information inflation vs fixed-n: 3.7934" is an artifact of the mislabeled design and must not be quoted anywhere — the true inflation for the intended design is ~1.05–1.10×. The prereg's framing ("gsDesign test.type=6 yields a differently-calibrated set; reconciling the two is a review-pass task") understates this: it is not a calibration difference, it is a different lower-boundary concept. Recompute canonically with test.type=4 and re-commit before ratification.

**4. SHOULD-FIX — Two-sided symmetric efficacy plus one-sided futility is directionally incoherent as written.**
The locked design stops on **either** boundary (the Python efficacy recursion at line 78 correctly zeroes mass outside ±u), so Z = −4.33 at look 1 is an efficacy crossing in the "same-vendor better" direction. But the provisional futility zone at look 1 is Z < −0.94, and the Python futility computation (line 114) masks only the **upper** efficacy bound. As written, a strong opposite-direction trend — itself a publishable finding — lands in the futility-recommendation zone and could be stopped as "no effect." Pre-register the precedence explicitly: futility recommendation applies only for Z ∈ (−b_k, a_k]; Z ≤ −b_k is a reverse-direction efficacy crossing and takes precedence. Since futility is non-binding, α bookkeeping is unaffected; this is a protocol-text fix plus one line in the script.

**5. SHOULD-FIX — The gatekeeping rule as stated does not control FWER for A1-vs-B in a group-sequential design.**
§3.6 says A1-vs-B "is formally tested only on a crossing of A2-vs-B," with no boundary specified — implying full α at the stopping look. It is an established result (Hung, Wang & O'Neill 2007; Tamhane, Mehta & Liu 2010; Glimm, Maurer & Bretz 2010) that testing a secondary hypothesis at the unadjusted level at the interim look where the primary crossed inflates the secondary's type-I error above α. Fix: pre-register that A1-vs-B, once unlocked, is tested against its **own** group-sequential boundary (the same sfLDOF schedule at the realized information at the stopping look) — or downgrade A1-vs-B to always-descriptive. One sentence now; unfixable after the fact.

**6. SHOULD-FIX — The Band-1 mechanical primary is contaminated by coincidental localization, and in a paired sparse-discordance design that contamination is not benign.**
The pilot's catch-audit overturned **2 of 2** mechanical catches (P-002 coincidental localization, P-003 inverted claim). Coincidental catches occur approximately independently across arms, so they generate discordant pairs that split ~50:50 — pure noise that (a) dilutes the discordant-pair odds ratio toward 1, cutting power at any true-detection delta below every computation above, and (b) can push the futility statistic around at small m. The endpoint choice is ruled (D-054a), so I am not re-litigating it; what the pre-registration should add: report the audited-catch discordant table at every look beside the mechanical one, pre-register the audit-overturn rate as a measured dilution parameter, and pre-register a sensitivity analysis of the primary result excluding audit-overturned catches. Cheap (audit is human time, not sessions) and it is the only defense if the mechanical and audited answers diverge at stopping.

**7. SHOULD-FIX — The estimation-at-stopping plan assumes machinery that is invalid at the realized sample sizes and targets the wrong estimand scale.**
Stagewise-ordering adjusted CIs and median-unbiased estimates (§3.7) are Brownian-model constructions; at m ≈ 5–15 discordant pairs they need to be computed on the **exact binomial sample space** (stagewise ordering is well-defined there; pre-register that implementation), not via normal approximation. Separately, the monitored statistic identifies the **conditional odds ratio / discordant-pair probability**, not the marginal risk difference — the adjusted estimate should be stated on that scale, with the risk difference reported descriptively using the concordant counts. Finally, the non-binding futility rule makes the stopping time partly discretionary: pre-register that adjusted inference is computed under the boundary actually followed, and that any supervisor override of a futility recommendation is logged as part of the inferential record (otherwise the adjusted CI's claimed coverage is unauditable).

**8. NOTE — Supply margin for n_max=44 is thinner than §2.3 reads.**
44 defects ≈ 380 screened rows against a 432-row post-gate pool means ~88% of the entire combined pool must be screened **if** the JS/TS-measured rates (29% usable × 40% yield) transfer — which §2.3 itself flags as unmeasured. If any single large tranche fails the D-055 validation gate (e.g., SWE-rebench's 110 Python rows), expected yield drops below 44 and the §2.5 own-harvest trigger fires. Treat own-harvest as the likely path, not the contingency; and note that if information is redefined in discordant pairs (finding 1), reaching m_max may require more cases than 44, making the administrative-cap/under-running rule the expected ending, not the exception.

**9. NOTE — Look 1 is ceremonial for efficacy and dangerous for futility.**
Efficacy at t=0.25 requires ≥18 one-way discordant pairs (impossible at n=11); the provisional futility bound (Z < −0.94) can be crossed by 2 discordant pairs both landing one way (Z = −1.41). The pre-registration should say plainly that look 1 exists for administrative/monitoring purposes, and gate futility (see proposal below).

**10. NOTE — Direction balance is load-bearing for the pooled paired estimate.**
A2-vs-B pooled over authoring directions conflates "cross-vendor effect" with vendor reviewer-skill asymmetry unless directions are balanced; D-056's created_at-ASC ordering leaves the language×vendor mix "where the dates land." The pilot alternated directions; the pre-registration should state the alternation rule explicitly in §3 so imbalance cannot arise, and keep the per-direction breakdown mandatory at every look.

**11. NOTE — Model drift interacts worse with a sequential design than with a fixed-n one.**
Accrual order is calendar time (created_at ASC, subscription stacks, no version pinning, ~16 weeks). A mid-study vendor model change is a time-confounder, and a design that stops on accumulated evidence weights early-calendar data more heavily under early stopping. The §4.5 config snapshot captures a change but does not neutralize it; pre-register that any observed stack change triggers a look-stratified (pre/post-change) sensitivity analysis.

**12. NOTE — Repeat-on-defective (§4.2) buys almost no information at these event rates.**
k=2 repeats on ~20% of 44 cases ≈ 18 sessions (>1 week of ceiling) re-running A2/B; at near-zero catch rates the expected number of catch events in the repeat set is ~0–2, so the catch-rate variance estimate the pilot failed to get will fail again, at higher cost. See cuts below.

### Futility calibration proposal (§3.4) — paste-ready block

> **§3.4a Futility calibration (pre-registered; rule remains non-binding per D-054e/D-058).**
>
> **Calibrating alternative.** δ* = 0.30 paired difference in Band-1 catch rate (A2 vs B), under the pre-registered nuisance model p_A2 = 0.05, p_B = 0.35 with cross-arm independence (discordance ≈ 0.365, expected m at n_max = 44 ≈ 16 discordant pairs; equivalently, conditional discordant-pair probability π* ≈ 0.90, odds ratio ≈ 9). *Justification:* the futility boundary must be drawn against the alternative the design can actually detect, and at I_max fixed by n_max = 44 the design's ~90%-power alternative is ≈30 pp with a near-zero same-vendor arm — which is also precisely the pilot's diff-anchoring regime (0/2 audited catches same-vendor; futility then means "cross-vendor rescue of the observed magnitude is ruled out"). Calibrating at a smaller δ (e.g. 20 pp, where realized power is only ~50–55%) would produce boundaries that recommend stopping on data fully consistent with a true 20 pp effect; effects in (0, 30 pp) are therefore addressed by the estimation report (adjusted CI at stopping), not by the futility rule, and this limitation of n_max is stated, not hidden.
>
> **Target power / β.** 1 − β = 0.90 (β = 0.10 spent by the futility boundary at δ*). *Justification:* the boundary's operating cost is the probability of wrongly recommending abandonment of a real δ*-sized effect; under a hard session ceiling that error wastes the entire study rather than a margin of it, so it is held to 10% rather than the conventional 20%. Because the rule is non-binding and escalated to a human (D-054e), realized power loss is strictly less than the nominal β; 0.10 also matches the committed provisional computation (θ_max = z_0.975 + z_0.90 = 3.2415), minimizing recomputation churn.
>
> **β-spending shape.** One-sided Lan-DeMets O'Brien-Fleming-type (sfLDOF) β-spending on the same information-fraction grid as the efficacy boundary, with a **minimum-information gate: no futility evaluation at any look with fewer than 8 observed discordant pairs** (unspent β carries forward automatically under the spending framework). *Justification:* sfLDOF spends ~5% of β at t = 0.25 and ~35% by t = 0.50, which places the real stopping opportunity at the mid-study look — where a stop still saves ≈ half the session budget — while keeping look 1, where the discordant count is expected to be 2–4 and the statistic is dominated by noise (a 2-pair fluke crosses the provisional look-1 bound), effectively closed via the information gate; using one spending family (sfLDOF) for both boundaries also keeps the design auditable in a single named-tool invocation. More aggressive shapes (Pocock-type, HSD γ > 0) buy little extra expected saving over sfLDOF at look 2 while materially raising the small-m false-futility risk that findings on sparse discordance identify as this design's weakest point.
>
> **Computation and lock.** Canonical: gsDesign 3.10.1, `gsDesign(k=4, test.type=4, alpha=0.025, beta=0.10, sfu=sfLDOF, sfl=sfLDOF, timing=c(.25,.5,.75,1))` (non-binding β-spending; supersedes the committed test.type=6 run, which computes an H0-spent lower bound and is not the ruled concept), cross-validated by the committed independent recursion; efficacy bounds must reproduce the LOCKED §3.3 constants unchanged. Futility applies only in the benefit direction: the recommendation zone is Z_k ∈ (−b_k, a_k]; Z_k ≤ −b_k is a reverse-direction efficacy crossing and takes precedence. At each eligible look the statistic is the exact conditional sign-test on discordant pairs (b of m), with Z_k = (2b − m)/√m compared to a_k. A crossing produces a written stop recommendation escalated to the supervisor (D-058 level 1/2); any override is logged with rationale as part of the inferential record.

### Add / cut under the 15-sessions/week ceiling

**Cut (sessions):**
- **Repeats (§4.2)** — cut, or make conditional: run only if the look-1 mechanical catch rate is ≥ ~20% (below that, k=2 repeats cannot measure catch-rate variance; they measure zeros). Reallocating ~18 sessions buys ~3 additional primary cases — worth more than an uninformative variance estimate.
- **A1 arm subsampling** — pre-register A1 on a 50% random subsample (seeded, D-027c discipline) from look 2 onward: saves ~1 session per affected case (5.5 → 4.5), i.e. ~2 extra weeks of primary accrual over the horizon. A1-vs-B is gated-secondary, structurally conservative (D-031e), and largely descriptive under finding 5; half-sample description preserves it.

**Add (all free — paper/code/human time, zero sessions):**
- The exact-conditional monitoring machinery and m-based information definition (finding 1) — this is the single highest-value addition and costs nothing.
- The corrected paired-exact power table and nuisance model (finding 2).
- The audited-catch discordant table at every look plus the audit-overturn dilution parameter (finding 6).
- The gatekeeping boundary for A1-vs-B, the futility precedence rule, and the alternation-rule statement (findings 4, 5, 10).
- A per-look data-freeze manifest (case IDs, m, b, stack snapshots) so each look is reconstructible.

**Do not add:** the semantic-catch layer (already correctly deferred — it multiplies judge calls, not information) and any new arm (OQ-23 model-tier); every session belongs to primary accrual.

**Bottom line:** the efficacy constants are verified correct and the sequential concept is the right one for this constraint set, but the package is not ratifiable as-is: findings 1 and 2 (information basis / exact-test degeneracy, and the false power claim) must be fixed in the pre-registration text and scripts, and findings 3–7 are one-sentence-to-one-script fixes that are only cheap before session 1.
